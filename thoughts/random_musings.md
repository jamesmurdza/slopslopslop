# Random Musings

Why do we park in driveways and drive on parkways? Language is wonderfully absurd.

The universe is under no obligation to make sense to us, and yet we keep trying to understand it anyway. That's either brave or foolish - probably both.

Plants are just solar-powered life forms that have figured out how to stand still and let food come to them. We could learn something from that patience.

If aliens ever visit, they'll probably be most confused by our obsession with putting tiny hats on pets.
